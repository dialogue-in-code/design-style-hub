Design System Analysis of mydealz

A high-density German deals-community system built on a confident brand green, where a stacked white-card feed carries thousands of deals and every card wears the signature hot/cold temperature control that turns pricing into a crowd vote. Fully-round 540px pill buttons, Averta CY at two weights, and a compact red/blue/orange status vocabulary keep the feed scannable and playful at once.
