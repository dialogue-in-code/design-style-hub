Design System Analysis of Oura

The dark-mode Oura system flips the paper: a warm espresso near-black canvas carries the same brown DNA, warm off-white text replaces the cream ink, and the earth-tone accents lift for legibility. The action blue and the two-typeface serif/grotesque voice carry over verbatim from light mode.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Two families carry the system: Editorial New, a light high-contrast serif for display and section headlines (Fraunces shown as substitute), and AkkuratLL, a neutral grotesque for reading (Inter shown as substitute). Scale creates hierarchy; display weights stay thin.

Touch targets: the pill CTAs and circular icon buttons clear 44×44 WCAG-AAA. Collapsing: nav → hamburger below the tablet breakpoint (blue CTA stays); multi-column pricing/feature grids reflow to a single stacked column; section padding compresses from section (128px) toward giant (64px); display serif scales down (110px → ~48px) while keeping its thin weight.
