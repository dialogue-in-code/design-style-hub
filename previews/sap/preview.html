Design System Analysis of SAP

An enterprise system built like an application frame rather than a marketing surface: a light chrome bar above a product tab rail, a canvas broken by four full-width flat bands, and one blue that does all the acting. Depth comes from band and gradient rather than shadow, and corners stop at 8px on controls and 12px on media.

02 — Foundations

Typography

72 Brand Variable is SAP's own variable typeface (Inter shown as substitute). What the variable axis buys is a weight range wider than most corporate systems use — 350, 400, 500 and 700 all appear — and the design leans on it hardest at the small end, where a 15px list item at 350 and a 15px nav link at 400 read as different classes of text at identical size. Tracking is left at zero everywhere.

Touch targets: button-floating clears 44×44 WCAG-AAA at 58px and button-primary meets it exactly at 44px; the inactive product tab sits at 32px. Collapsing: the global bar collapses its links behind a menu but keeps the logo, search and account affordances; the product tab rail becomes a horizontal scroller so the active tab stays anchored; two-column features stack and the 2x2 grid keeps its hairlines; bands hold their full-bleed width at every size while their inner padding compresses.
