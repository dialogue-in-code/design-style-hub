Design System Analysis of Oracle

An enterprise system built on a warm neutral ladder rather than a colour palette. Six rungs carry almost every surface, a single charcoal carries every action, and the famous red is held back for the wordmark alone. Two display registers coexist, being a very light sans on the corporate index and a Georgia serif across the product pages.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Two display registers run side by side — Oracle Sans at weight 300 on the corporate index, and Georgia across the product pages. Beneath both, Oracle Sans handles everything else, dropping to 350 for running copy and jumping to 800 for section headings, so hierarchy is carried by weight far more than by size. Letter-spacing is zero on every role at every size. Inter stands in for Oracle Sans; Georgia is shown as itself.

12 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

The system carries no shadows at all. Depth is produced entirely by position on the neutral ladder, and where two surfaces share a rung a 1px hairline does the separating instead.

Level 0 — flat fill on the ladder. Every band, section, nav and footer.
Level 1 — one rung up or down. Cards on a tinted band, the deep sub-navigation strip.
Level 2 — a 1px hairline where two surfaces share a rung. Navigation tiles and disabled controls.

Touch targets: button-primary stands 37px tall at its 10px vertical padding and relies on surrounding margin; text-input and product-subnav clear the 44×44 WCAG AAA minimum at roughly 46px and 64px. Raising the button's vertical padding to 14px brings it to 45px without changing any other value. Collapsing: the masthead search field collapses to an icon below tablet while the rest holds position; the sub-navigation keeps its full-bleed band at every width and scrolls horizontally rather than wrapping, so the active gold bar stays visible; card grids step 4-up to 3-up to 2-up to 1-up; the channel columns drop their vertical rules when they stack; section padding holds at 48px far longer than most systems would keep it.
