Design System Analysis of Al Jazeera

A hard-news editorial system on flat paper: dense Roboto headline stacks, hairline rules instead of shadows, and photography doing the visual work. One saturated orange signs the masthead logo block and every section-eyebrow bar; a dedicated live-red marks breaking coverage with badges, bands, and timeline dots; actions are compact black pills. The corporate network pages carry the same identity onto a near-black canvas with royal-indigo panels, gold rules, amber links, and teal and crimson section rails.

Touch targets: pills pad 6×17 around 16px labels — keep row spacing so targets clear 44×44; timeline items and ranked rows are full-width tap zones. Collapsing: the nav collapses to hamburger while the logo block and LIVE pill persist; news grids collapse column-by-column to the mobile feed; the live timeline keeps its dot rail at every width; network portrait grids step 4 → 2 → 1 with rails moving above the grid.
