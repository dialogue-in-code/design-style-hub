NerdWallet 设计分析

NerdWallet 是穿着编辑外套的比价引擎。文章页以宽阔的绿色渐变带开场，随后落入近白区域，致密的利率表格在那里做真正的工作。一种深绿填满所有按钮、激活标签与评分星；其下是三阶薄荷色阶梯洗刷着区块面板。圆角几乎为零，基本没有投影。

The green action. Flat 16px on all four sides and a 2px radius keep it reading as a rectangle.

08 — 基础规范

阴影与层级

Level 0 — flat on the canvas. Body copy, table rows, headings.
Level 1 — 1px hairline rule. Row dividers and spec-cell separators.
Level 2 — 1px strong border. Product cards, outline buttons, accordion rows.
Level 3 — two stacked 5% shadows. Registers as settling, not lifting.
Level 4 — overlay surfaces.

Touch targets: the filled button pair clears 44×44 comfortably at its flat 16px padding; button-outline at 8px 16px sits close to the minimum and needs its hit area extended on touch widths. Collapsing: the nav keeps the wordmark, search and both account controls as long as possible and drops the category links first. Comparison tables are the main responsive event — each row reflows into a stacked card where the institution, rating and rate become vertical fields and the action goes full-width.
