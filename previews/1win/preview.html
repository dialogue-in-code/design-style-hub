Design System Analysis of 1win

A near-black crypto casino and sportsbook where two action colors carry everything — a vivid green for Registration and primary confirms, an electric blue for secondary actions and live betting. Inter is set at heavy weights, the surface ladder is a flat dark grey, and dense rails of full-bleed game tiles supply all the colour.

Touch targets: buttons carry 8×12px padding; the green Registration CTA is the largest tap surface. Collapsing: the left icon rail collapses first to a bottom/hamburger nav; tab strips and odds rows become horizontally scrollable; game rails keep their carousel and show fewer tiles; the betslip docks right on desktop, becomes a bottom sheet on mobile.
