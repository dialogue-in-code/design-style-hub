Design System Analysis of DJI

DJI in dark mode, sourced from DESIGN-DARK.md: the same product showroom with the house lights down. Studio renders sit in square tiles on a neutral near-black canvas, Open Sans is set in soft light ink at stepped opacities, and every action keeps its exact DJI blue pill.

Touch targets: store CTAs and the large outline pill are 48px tall, card pills 40px, nav pills 32px with generous horizontal padding. Collapsing: the nav collapses to a menu with the Store pill kept; two-up tiles and field cards stack; the comparison table scrolls horizontally; the store gallery stacks above the purchase column while the sticky purchase bar keeps price and both CTAs reachable.
